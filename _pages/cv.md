## Academic Profile

Professor of Mechanical Engineering with research and professional experience spanning computational mechanics, contact mechanics, fracture mechanics, thin-film and surface mechanics, crashworthiness, structural optimization, and finite element analysis. My work combines analytical modeling, numerical simulation, optimization, and experimental validation, with applications ranging from advanced materials and thin films to transportation structures and mechanical systems.

## Graduate Research Supervision

My graduate supervision has included Ph.D. and M.S. research in computational mechanics, peridynamics, structural optimization, fatigue, crashworthiness, composite materials, and mechanical system design.

Selected doctoral research has addressed

- power-split infinitely variable transmissions,
- peridynamic fracture mechanics,
- fatigue life prediction of welded structures,
- crashworthiness and energy absorption.

Graduate research has resulted in journal publications, conference papers, computational methodologies, and university–industry research collaborations.
