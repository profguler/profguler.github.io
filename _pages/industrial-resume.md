## Technical Expertise

- **Mechanical integrity:** Structural stress analysis, fatigue and durability, fracture mechanics, failure investigation, welded structures, and design evaluation.
- **Finite element simulation:** ANSYS, LS-DYNA, Abaqus, ANSA; linear and nonlinear FEA, contact, friction, material nonlinearity, large deformation, and buckling.
- **Thermal and dynamic analysis:** Coupled thermal-structural response, structural dynamics, vibration, modal analysis, and transient loading.
- **Validation and engineering methods:** Strain-gauge correlation, static and fatigue testing, model verification, parametric analysis, simulation automation, and technical reporting.

## Industrial Engineering Experience

### CD-adapco Inc. (now Siemens) — Senior Structural & Thermal Engineer
*Melville, NY | September 2000 – September 2004*

Performed structural, thermal, dynamic, nonlinear, and coupled thermomechanical finite element analyses for industrial equipment, gas-turbine and compressor components, engines, and automotive applications. Developed ANSYS models of a glass-lined industrial reactor; evaluated stresses and critical regions. Investigated crack-prone regions in diesel-engine and EGR cooler components and recommended design improvements.

### TEMSA Global Inc. — Chief Structural Analysis Engineer
*Adana, Türkiye | March 2005 – September 2006*

Led structural CAE work, including full-vehicle FE modeling with ANSA, nonlinear crash simulations with LS-DYNA, and structural, dynamic, and modal analyses using ANSYS.
