# Mehmet A. Guler, Ph.D.

**Professor of Mechanical Engineering**

*Computational Mechanics • Engineering Design • Finite Element Analysis • Crashworthiness*

## Biography

I am a Professor of Mechanical Engineering with more than twenty years of academic experience and seven years of professional engineering experience. My work spans computational mechanics, contact mechanics, crashworthiness, structural mechanics, finite element analysis, lightweight structures, and simulation-driven engineering design.

I currently serve as Professor of Mechanical Engineering at the American University of the Middle East. Previously, I held faculty appointments at TOBB University of Economics and Technology and worked in industry as a Senior Structural and Thermal Engineer at CD-adapco, now Siemens Digital Industries Software, and as a Senior Structural Engineer at TEMSA. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Lehigh University.

My teaching and research seek to connect rigorous engineering fundamentals with computational methods, industrial practice, and open-ended engineering design. I have taught courses ranging from statics, dynamics, and mechanics of materials to machine design, finite element analysis, system modeling, and senior capstone design.
