**Professor of Mechanical Engineering**  
**American University of the Middle East**

*Computational Mechanics • Contact Mechanics • Engineering Design • Finite Element Analysis*

## Biography

I am a Professor of Mechanical Engineering with more than twenty years of academic experience and seven years of professional engineering experience. My work focuses on computational mechanics, contact mechanics, crashworthiness, structural mechanics, finite element analysis, lightweight structures, and simulation-driven engineering design.

Before joining the American University of the Middle East, I held faculty appointments at TOBB University of Economics and Technology. My industrial experience includes positions as Senior Structural and Thermal Engineer at CD-adapco, now part of Siemens Digital Industries Software, and Senior Structural Engineer at TEMSA. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Lehigh University.

My teaching and research connect rigorous engineering fundamentals with computational methods, industrial practice, and open-ended engineering design. I have taught courses ranging from statics, dynamics, and mechanics of materials to machine design, finite element analysis, system modeling, and senior capstone design.
