<div class="research-section">

<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>

My research in crashworthiness focuses on the design and optimization of lightweight energy-absorbing structures capable of improving occupant safety while minimizing structural weight. By combining structural mechanics, nonlinear finite element analysis, optimization techniques, and engineering design, I investigate the crash performance of thin-walled metallic structures subjected to impact loading.

</p>

<p>

Particular emphasis is placed on corrugated multi-cell crash boxes, progressive collapse mechanisms, lightweight structural concepts, surrogate modeling, and simulation-driven design methodologies for automotive and transportation engineering applications.

</p>

</div>

<div class="research-section">

<h2>Current Research</h2>

<p>

Current work focuses on optimizing corrugated multi-cell crash boxes using nonlinear finite element simulations, Latin Hypercube Sampling, XGBoost surrogate models, and NSGA-II multi-objective optimization. The objective is to maximize specific energy absorption while minimizing peak crushing force and structural mass.

</p>
