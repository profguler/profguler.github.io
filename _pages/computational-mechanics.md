<!--                    OVERVIEW                               -->
<!-- ========================================================= -->

<div class="research-section">

  <h2>Overview</h2>

  <p>
  My research in computational mechanics focuses on the development and application of numerical methods for solving complex engineering problems involving structural response, manufacturing processes, multiphysics interactions, fatigue, and design optimization.
  </p>

  <p>
  Particular emphasis is placed on finite element analysis, nonlinear simulation, computational model verification, fluid–structure interaction, structural connections, manufacturing mechanics, and surrogate-assisted design. This work is supported by extensive experience in both academic research and industrial computer-aided engineering.
  </p>

</div>

<!-- ========================================================= -->
<!--                    CURRENT RESEARCH                       -->
<!-- ========================================================= -->

<div class="research-section">

  <h2>Current Research</h2>

  <p>
  Current work focuses on combining high-fidelity finite element simulation with efficient surrogate models, machine learning, and multi-objective optimization. These methods are being applied to structural design, crashworthiness, thermal interfaces, composite systems, and reliability assessment.
  </p>

  <p>
  Additional work addresses computational model reduction, verification, uncertainty, and the development of practical simulation workflows that connect analytical mechanics, numerical methods, and engineering design.
  </p>

</div>
