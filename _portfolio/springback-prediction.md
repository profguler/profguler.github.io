## Project Overview

This TÜBİTAK-funded research project focused on the accurate prediction and robust optimization of springback in advanced high-strength dual-phase steels during sheet metal forming operations. The research combined nonlinear finite element analysis, experimental validation, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, sensitivity analysis, and robust optimization techniques to improve dimensional accuracy while reducing springback variability in automotive sheet metal components.

Conducted in collaboration with **Coşkunöz Holding**, the project addressed one of the major manufacturing challenges associated with advanced high-strength steels, whose increased strength results in significantly larger springback than conventional steels. The developed methodologies enabled accurate prediction of springback while simultaneously minimizing both the mean springback and its variation caused by uncertainties in material properties and manufacturing processes.

---

## Highlights

- Developed validated finite element models using **LS-DYNA** for predicting springback in sheet metal forming operations.
- Investigated benchmark forming problems including U-channel, 7-flange, and S-rail geometries.
- Evaluated the influence of constitutive material models including Hill48, Barlat89, and YLD2000.
- Performed comprehensive experimental validation of numerical springback predictions.
- Developed surrogate models using response surface methodology, Kriging, radial basis functions, and polynomial regression.
- Proposed deterministic and robust optimization methodologies for minimizing springback.
- Developed a double-loop Monte Carlo simulation framework to quantify part-to-part and batch-to-batch springback variability.
- Demonstrated significant reductions in both springback magnitude and variability through robust optimization.

## Industrial Collaboration

The project included extensive collaboration with **Coşkunöz Holding**, one of Turkey's leading automotive tooling and manufacturing companies. Experimental forming tools were designed and manufactured specifically for the project, enabling comprehensive validation of finite element simulations and optimization methodologies under industrially relevant manufacturing conditions.

---

## Project Impact

This project established one of the first comprehensive research frameworks in Turkey integrating finite element simulation, experimental validation, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, Monte Carlo simulation, sensitivity analysis, deterministic optimization, and robust optimization for springback prediction and compensation in advanced high-strength steels. The methodologies developed in this project resulted in two journal articles, one international conference paper, one international conference presentation, two M.S. theses, and provided the foundation for subsequent research on uncertainty quantification and robust design optimization in computational mechanics.
