## Project Overview

This TÜBİTAK-funded university–industry collaborative research project focused on the design and structural optimization of a new generation **100% low-floor tram vehicle** for urban rail transportation. The project was carried out jointly by **Hacettepe University** and **Bozankaya A.Ş.**, one of Turkey's leading manufacturers of rail transportation systems.

The primary objective was to develop a lightweight, structurally efficient, and crashworthy tram body and chassis using advanced finite element methods while satisfying European railway design standards. Comprehensive numerical analyses were performed under static, fatigue, operational, and crash loading conditions to optimize the vehicle structure before prototype manufacturing.

The project resulted in the successful development of a production-ready tram platform and established computational methodologies for structural analysis and optimization of railway vehicles.

## Research Objectives

The project aimed to

- develop the structural design of a new generation low-floor tram vehicle,
- construct high-fidelity finite element models of the body and chassis,
- perform static structural analyses according to EN 12663,
- perform fatigue assessment according to VDV 152,
- evaluate crashworthiness according to EN 15227,
- optimize the chassis and body structures for weight reduction while maintaining structural integrity,
- verify compliance with international railway standards prior to prototype production.

---

## Technical Contributions

Major technical achievements include

- Developed detailed finite element models of the complete tram body and chassis.
- Performed static analyses under operational loading conditions.
- Conducted fatigue analyses of welded structures according to VDV 152.
- Performed crashworthiness analyses according to EN 15227.
- Optimized structural members for reduced weight and improved stiffness.
- Evaluated passenger loading, load transfer, and curving performance.
- Assisted in the development of a production-ready 100% low-floor tram prototype.
- Established numerical procedures that were later used in subsequent tram development projects.

## Industrial Collaboration

This project was conducted in close collaboration with **Bozankaya A.Ş.**, a leading Turkish manufacturer of rail transportation systems.

The university research team developed detailed finite element models and performed structural optimization studies, while the industrial partner supplied CAD models, design requirements, manufacturing constraints, and prototype validation.

The project significantly contributed to the development of one of Turkey's first domestically designed low-floor tram platforms.

---

## Project Impact

The project successfully demonstrated the application of advanced computational mechanics techniques to the design of modern railway vehicles.

Major outcomes include

- development of a production-ready tram body and chassis,
- implementation of advanced finite element methodologies for railway structures,
- optimization of structural weight while satisfying international standards,
- one international journal article,
- one international conference paper,
- one M.S. thesis,
- establishment of long-term university–industry collaboration with Bozankaya A.Ş.

The methodologies developed during this project subsequently supported further research on fatigue assessment, lightweight transportation structures, and railway vehicle crashworthiness.
