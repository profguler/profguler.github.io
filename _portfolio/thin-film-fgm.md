## Project Overview

This TÜBİTAK-funded research project focused on developing analytical and numerical methods for the contact mechanics analysis of functionally graded materials (FGMs). The research investigated stress concentrations and singularities in thin films, cover plates, and graded coatings subjected to thermal, mechanical, and residual loading. Analytical formulations based on singular integral equations were developed and validated using finite element analysis to better understand crack initiation, interfacial delamination, and structural reliability in advanced layered materials.

---

## Highlights

- Developed analytical contact mechanics formulations for functionally graded materials.
- Derived singular integral equation solutions for thin films and cover plates bonded to graded substrates.
- Investigated stress concentrations and singularities responsible for crack initiation and interfacial delamination.
- Studied the influence of material gradation, geometry, thermal loading, and residual stresses.
- Validated analytical solutions using finite element analysis.
- Produced two journal articles, two international conference papers, and one M.S. thesis.
