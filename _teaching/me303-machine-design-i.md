## Course Overview

ME 303 introduces the design of mechanical components using stress analysis, failure theories, material selection, safety factors, and engineering design principles. The course connects mechanics and materials knowledge to practical machine-component design.

## Course Content

- Mechanical design methodology
- Material selection and design factors
- Stress concentration
- Static failure theories
- Fatigue fundamentals
- Bolted and welded joints
- Springs
- Shafts and basic power-transmission components

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Apply a systematic approach to machine-component design.
2. Determine stresses and deformations under representative loading conditions.
3. Apply appropriate failure criteria and safety factors.
4. Account for material properties and stress concentrations.
5. Design common joints, springs, and basic machine elements.
6. Evaluate designs for strength, stiffness, and manufacturability.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.
