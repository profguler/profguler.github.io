## Course Overview

ME 270 provides a foundation in engineering mechanics through the analysis of particles and rigid bodies. Emphasis is placed on free-body diagrams, equilibrium, force systems, moments, and the systematic solution of mechanics problems.

## Course Content

- Vectors and force systems
- Equilibrium of particles
- Moments and couples
- Equivalent force-couple systems
- Rigid-body equilibrium
- Free-body diagrams
- Trusses, frames, and machines
- Internal forces
- Friction
- Centroids and area moments of inertia

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Represent forces and moments using vectors.
2. Construct accurate free-body diagrams.
3. Apply equilibrium equations to particles and rigid bodies.
4. Analyze trusses, frames, and machines.
5. Determine internal forces and friction effects.
6. Calculate centroids and area moments of inertia.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.
