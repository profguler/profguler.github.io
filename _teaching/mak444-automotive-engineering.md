## Course Overview

MAK 444 introduces the application of mechanical engineering principles to vehicle systems, performance, structural design, safety, and component integration. The course connects dynamics, mechanics, design, and energy concepts to road-vehicle engineering.

## Course Content

- Vehicle architecture and major subsystems
- Longitudinal vehicle dynamics
- Braking and traction
- Ride and handling fundamentals
- Powertrain and transmission systems
- Vehicle structural loads
- Crashworthiness and occupant safety
- Materials and lightweight design
- Automotive testing and performance evaluation
- Design tradeoffs in vehicle engineering

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Identify the functions and interactions of major vehicle subsystems.
2. Analyze basic acceleration, braking, and traction performance.
3. Apply dynamics and mechanics principles to vehicle behavior.
4. Evaluate structural loads and safety considerations.
5. Compare material and design alternatives for automotive applications.
6. Communicate engineering evaluations of vehicle systems.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.

## Assessment Methods

- Homework
- Quizzes
- Midterm examination
- Vehicle-system analysis assignments
- Final examination
