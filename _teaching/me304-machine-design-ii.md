## Course Overview

ME 304 extends machine-design principles to advanced mechanical components and power-transmission systems. The course emphasizes fatigue, shafts, bearings, gears, clutches, brakes, and the integration of components into complete systems.

## Course Content

- Fatigue design
- Shaft design
- Rolling-contact bearings
- Journal bearings
- Gears and gear trains
- Clutches and brakes
- Belt and chain drives
- Mechanical power-transmission systems

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Apply fatigue criteria to components under fluctuating loading.
2. Design shafts and select bearings.
3. Analyze and design gears and gear trains.
4. Design or select clutches, brakes, belts, and chains.
5. Use standards and catalog data in engineering design.
6. Integrate machine elements into a reliable system.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.

## Assessment Methods

- Homework
- Quizzes
- Midterm examination
- Design problems
- Final examination
