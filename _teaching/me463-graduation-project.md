ME 463 is a senior-level capstone design course in which students apply engineering principles to the development of a complete mechanical engineering project. Students work in teams to identify a problem, develop design alternatives, conduct engineering analyses, construct or evaluate prototypes, and communicate their results.

## Main Topics

- Engineering design methodology
- Problem definition and project planning
- Concept generation and selection
- Mechanical component and system design
- Engineering analysis and simulation
- Material and manufacturing-process selection
- Prototype development and testing
- Project management
- Engineering ethics and professional responsibility
- Technical reporting and oral presentation

## Teaching Approach

The course is organized around project-based learning. Students are guided through the complete engineering design process while being encouraged to make independent technical decisions and justify their choices using analysis, experimentation, and engineering standards.

Regular project meetings are used to evaluate progress, identify technical difficulties, and strengthen teamwork, communication, and project-management skills.

## Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Apply the engineering design process to an open-ended problem.
2. Develop and evaluate alternative design concepts.
3. Conduct analytical, numerical, and experimental investigations.
4. Work effectively as members of a multidisciplinary team.
5. Consider safety, sustainability, ethics, and economic constraints.
6. Prepare professional engineering reports and presentations.

## Assessment Methods

- Project proposal
- Design reviews
- Progress reports
- Engineering analyses
- Prototype or computational model
- Final technical report
- Oral presentation
- Individual and team performance
