## Course Overview

ME 463 is a senior-level capstone course in which students integrate knowledge from across the mechanical engineering curriculum to address an open-ended engineering problem. Emphasis is placed on the complete design process, including problem definition, concept generation, engineering analysis, design evaluation, prototyping, testing, project management, teamwork, and professional communication.

## Course Content

- Engineering design methodology and problem definition
- Customer needs, engineering requirements, and design constraints
- Concept generation, screening, and selection
- Analytical, numerical, and experimental design evaluation
- Materials and manufacturing-process selection
- Design for safety, reliability, sustainability, and cost
- Prototype development, testing, and validation
- Project planning, teamwork, and professional responsibility
- Technical reports, design reviews, and oral presentations

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Apply the engineering design process to a complex, open-ended problem.
2. Develop and compare alternative design concepts using appropriate engineering criteria.
3. Perform analytical, computational, and experimental evaluations of a proposed design.
4. Incorporate safety, economic, environmental, ethical, and manufacturability considerations.
5. Function effectively as a member or leader of an engineering team.
6. Communicate design decisions and results through professional reports and presentations.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.

## Assessment Methods

- Project proposal and planning documents
- Conceptual and detailed design reviews
- Engineering analyses and design calculations
- Prototype, simulation model, or experimental validation
- Progress reports and team performance
- Final technical report and oral presentation
