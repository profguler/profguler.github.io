CVL 297 introduces the principles of engineering mechanics for particles and rigid bodies in static equilibrium. The course provides the foundation required for later study in solid mechanics, structural analysis, machine design, and dynamics.

## Main Topics

- Vectors and force systems
- Equilibrium of particles
- Moments and couples
- Equilibrium of rigid bodies
- Free-body diagrams
- Structural analysis
- Trusses
- Frames and machines
- Internal forces
- Friction
- Centroids and centers of gravity
- Area moments of inertia

## Teaching Approach

The course emphasizes the correct construction of free-body diagrams and the systematic application of equilibrium equations. Students are encouraged to connect mathematical calculations with the physical behavior of structures and mechanical systems.

Worked examples progress from fundamental force systems to practical engineering structures, frames, and machines.

## Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Resolve forces into Cartesian components.
2. Draw accurate free-body diagrams.
3. Apply equilibrium equations to particles and rigid bodies.
4. Analyze trusses, frames, and machines.
5. Determine internal forces in structural members.
6. Calculate centroids and area moments of inertia.
