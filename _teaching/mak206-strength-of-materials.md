## Course Overview

MAK 206 examines the response of deformable bodies to axial, torsional, bending, shear, and combined loading. Students develop the ability to calculate stress, strain, deformation, and stability limits for engineering components.

## Course Content

- Stress, strain, and constitutive behavior
- Axial loading
- Torsion
- Shear-force and bending-moment diagrams
- Bending and transverse shear stresses
- Beam deflection
- Combined loading
- Stress transformation
- Pressure vessels
- Column buckling

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Calculate stress and deformation under basic loading conditions.
2. Construct shear-force and bending-moment diagrams.
3. Analyze beams, shafts, vessels, and columns.
4. Apply stress-transformation relations.
5. Evaluate components under combined loading.
6. Assess strength, stiffness, and stability.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.
