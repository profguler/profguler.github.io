ME 200 introduces the fundamental principles of engineering thermodynamics and their application to energy-conversion systems. The course develops students’ ability to analyze thermodynamic properties, processes, and cycles using the laws of thermodynamics.

## Main Topics

- Thermodynamic systems and properties
- Energy, heat, and work
- Properties of pure substances
- Property tables and diagrams
- Ideal-gas relations
- Conservation of mass
- First law of thermodynamics
- Closed-system energy analysis
- Control-volume analysis
- Second law of thermodynamics
- Entropy
- Introduction to power and refrigeration cycles

## Teaching Approach

The course combines fundamental theory with practical engineering applications. Students are taught to define systems carefully, identify energy interactions, select appropriate property relations, and apply conservation laws systematically.

Engineering examples involving turbines, compressors, pumps, heat exchangers, boilers, condensers, and refrigeration devices are used throughout the course.

## Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Determine thermodynamic properties of pure substances.
2. Apply mass and energy conservation to engineering systems.
3. Analyze closed systems and steady-flow devices.
4. Apply the first and second laws of thermodynamics.
5. Calculate entropy changes and evaluate process feasibility.
6. Analyze basic power and refrigeration cycles.
