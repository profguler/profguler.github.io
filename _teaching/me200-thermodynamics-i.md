## Course Overview

ME 200 introduces the principles of engineering thermodynamics and their application to energy-conversion systems. Students learn to evaluate thermodynamic properties and apply mass, energy, and entropy balances to closed systems and control volumes.

## Course Content

- Thermodynamic systems, properties, and state
- Heat, work, and energy
- Properties of pure substances
- Property tables and thermodynamic diagrams
- Ideal-gas relations
- Conservation of mass and energy
- Closed-system analysis
- Steady-flow devices
- The second law of thermodynamics
- Entropy and introduction to engineering cycles

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Determine thermodynamic properties using tables and equations of state.
2. Apply mass and energy balances to closed and open systems.
3. Analyze turbines, compressors, pumps, nozzles, and heat exchangers.
4. Apply the second law and calculate entropy changes.
5. Evaluate the feasibility and efficiency of thermodynamic processes.
6. Analyze introductory power and refrigeration cycles.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.
