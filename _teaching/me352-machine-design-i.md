ME 352 introduces the principles of mechanical engineering design and the analysis of fundamental machine components. The course integrates mechanics of materials, materials selection, failure theories, and design methodology.

## Main Topics

- Mechanical design process
- Materials and manufacturing considerations
- Stress and deformation analysis
- Static failure theories
- Stress concentration
- Design for strength and stiffness
- Bolted and threaded joints
- Welded joints
- Springs
- Introduction to fatigue design

## Teaching Approach

The course emphasizes systematic problem solving and engineering judgment. Students begin with the identification of loads and constraints and proceed through stress analysis, failure assessment, material selection, and determination of suitable dimensions.

Practical examples are used to show how theoretical mechanics supports the design of safe and reliable mechanical components.

## Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Apply a structured mechanical-design process.
2. Determine stresses and deformations in machine components.
3. Apply appropriate static-failure criteria.
4. Account for stress concentrations and safety factors.
5. Design bolted, welded, and spring components.
6. Select suitable materials for mechanical applications.

## Assessment Methods

- Design exercises

- Component-design project
