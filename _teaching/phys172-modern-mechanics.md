PHYS 172 develops a calculus-based understanding of classical mechanics with emphasis on physical modeling, vector reasoning, conservation principles, and the analysis of particle systems.

## Main Topics

- Position, velocity, and acceleration
- Vector representation of motion
- Newton’s laws of motion
- Force and momentum
- Work and kinetic energy
- Potential energy
- Conservation of energy
- Linear momentum and collisions
- Angular momentum
- Rotational motion
- Oscillations
- Gravitational interactions

## Teaching Approach

The course emphasizes conceptual understanding together with mathematical analysis. Physical situations are represented using diagrams, vectors, equations, and computational models.

Students are encouraged to interpret the physical meaning of their solutions rather than treating mechanics as a collection of formulas.

## Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Represent motion using vectors and mathematical models.
2. Apply Newton’s laws to particle systems.
3. Use momentum and energy methods to solve mechanics problems.
4. Analyze translational and rotational motion.
5. Apply conservation laws to physical systems.
6. Interpret mathematical results in terms of physical behavior.
