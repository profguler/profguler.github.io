## Course Overview

CVL 340 applies fluid-mechanics principles to civil and environmental engineering systems. The course emphasizes the analysis and design of pressurized pipe systems, open-channel flow, hydraulic machinery, and practical water-conveyance systems.

## Course Content

- Review of conservation laws for fluid flow
- Pipe flow and energy losses
- Series, parallel, and networked pipe systems
- Pumps and system curves
- Flow measurement
- Open-channel flow fundamentals
- Specific energy and critical flow
- Uniform flow and gradually varied flow
- Hydraulic jumps
- Hydraulic-system analysis and design

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Apply conservation of mass, momentum, and energy to hydraulic systems.
2. Calculate losses and flow rates in pipe systems.
3. Analyze pump and piping-system performance.
4. Classify and evaluate open-channel flow.
5. Determine critical and uniform-flow conditions.
6. Analyze practical hydraulic engineering systems.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.
