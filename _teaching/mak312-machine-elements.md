## Course Overview

MAK 312 develops the analysis and design of common machine elements using mechanics, materials, failure criteria, fatigue, safety factors, and engineering standards.

## Course Content

- Mechanical design process and design factors
- Stress concentration and failure theories
- Fatigue design
- Bolted and welded joints
- Springs
- Shafts and keys
- Bearings
- Gears
- Clutches and brakes
- Power-transmission components

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Analyze machine elements under static and fluctuating loads.
2. Apply appropriate failure criteria and safety factors.
3. Design joints, springs, shafts, and related components.
4. Select bearings and power-transmission components.
5. Use engineering standards and catalog information.
6. Evaluate component designs for safety and reliability.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.
