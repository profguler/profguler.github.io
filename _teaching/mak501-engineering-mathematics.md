## Course Overview

MAK 501 presents advanced mathematical methods used in engineering analysis and mechanics. The course develops the ability to formulate and solve differential equations, eigenvalue problems, transform-based problems, and related analytical models.

## Course Content

- Linear algebra and eigenvalue problems
- Ordinary differential equations
- Series solutions and special functions
- Laplace and Fourier transforms
- Fourier series
- Partial differential equations
- Boundary- and initial-value problems
- Sturm-Liouville theory
- Complex-variable methods
- Applications in mechanics and engineering science

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Formulate engineering problems using advanced mathematical models.
2. Solve linear systems and eigenvalue problems.
3. Apply transform and series methods to differential equations.
4. Classify and solve representative partial differential equations.
5. Apply boundary and initial conditions to engineering models.
6. Interpret mathematical solutions within an engineering context.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.

## Assessment Methods

- Homework and analytical problem sets
- Midterm examination
- Applied mathematics assignments
- Final examination
