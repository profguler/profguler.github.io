## Course Overview

ME 309 introduces the fundamental principles governing fluids at rest and in motion. Students apply conservation of mass, momentum, and energy to analyze internal and external flows and common engineering flow devices.

## Course Content

- Fluid properties and dimensional analysis
- Fluid statics and hydrostatic forces
- Kinematics of fluid motion
- Control-volume analysis
- Continuity, momentum, and energy equations
- Bernoulli equation and engineering applications
- Internal flow and head losses
- Pipe networks and flow measurement
- External flow and drag
- Introduction to turbomachinery

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Determine pressure distributions and hydrostatic forces.
2. Apply conservation laws to fluid-flow systems.
3. Analyze pipe flow and calculate major and minor losses.
4. Use dimensional analysis and similarity principles.
5. Evaluate forces generated by fluid momentum changes.
6. Interpret fluid-mechanics results in engineering applications.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.
