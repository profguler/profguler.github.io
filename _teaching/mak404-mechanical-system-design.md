## Course Overview

MAK 404 integrates engineering analysis and design in the development of mechanical systems. Students work through problem definition, concept development, component selection, system modeling, design evaluation, and technical communication.

## Course Content

- Engineering design process
- Functional decomposition and requirements
- Concept generation and selection
- Mechanical-system modeling
- Component sizing and selection
- Design for reliability and safety
- Manufacturing and cost considerations
- Design optimization and tradeoff analysis
- Team-based project management
- Technical reporting and presentations

## Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:

1. Translate needs into engineering requirements and constraints.
2. Generate and evaluate alternative system concepts.
3. Integrate mechanical components into a complete design.
4. Apply analysis and simulation to support design decisions.
5. Consider safety, cost, manufacturing, and reliability.
6. Communicate system-design results in written and oral form.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

The course combines clear presentation of fundamental principles with worked engineering examples, structured problem solving, and design or analysis activities appropriate to the subject. Emphasis is placed on connecting mathematical formulations to physical behavior, checking the reasonableness of results, and communicating engineering decisions clearly.

## Assessment Methods

- Design assignments
- Project planning and reviews
- Engineering analyses
- Team project
- Final report and presentation
